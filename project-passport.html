UX Research · Grad School Project

US Passport Services — Travel.gov

ContextGraduate coursework, M.S. Human-Computer Interaction
FocusMixed-methods user research
RoleUX Researcher

Overview

A user research study of the U.S. passport services experience on Travel.gov, undertaken as part of graduate coursework in the Human-Computer Interaction program at the University of Maryland.

Approach

Conducted user testing of the platform using mixed research methods to surface where the site was falling short for the people trying to use it — from navigation and findability to task completion around passport applications and renewals.

Outcome

Synthesized research findings into a set of proposed user experience and design changes aimed at addressing the platform's core usability weaknesses.
