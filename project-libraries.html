Libraries and Their Proposed Impact with Open Data

ContextGraduate coursework, M.S. Human-Computer Interaction
FormatResearch paper
FocusCivic technology & Open Data

Overview

A research paper examining the impact of public libraries in underrepresented communities, and how those libraries can leverage Open Data to better serve their constituents.

Focus Areas

The research explored the intersection of civic institutions and public data access — looking at how libraries, as trusted community touchpoints, are positioned to translate Open Data into meaningful services for the populations that need them most.
